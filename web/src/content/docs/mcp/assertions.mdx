---
title: Assertions & Perf
description: Assert state, not just act.
---

`browser_assert` kinds: `visible, hidden, exists, text, value, contains, enabled, disabled, checked, count` + operators `equals|contains|gte|lte|gt|lt|regex`.

`browser_assert_perf` metrics: `fcp, load, ttfb, domContentLoaded` vs budget `lte|lt|gte|gt|equals`.

Failures become `ASSERTION_FAILURE` issues in the report.
