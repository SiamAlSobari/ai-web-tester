---
title: Browser Control
description: Open, act, inspect, tabs, auth.
---

`browser_open` launches `chromium|firefox|webkit`, device presets (iPhone 15 etc), viewport, networkProfile `None|Fast 3G|Slow 3G|Offline`, trace/video/HAR.

`browser_act` actions: `click, fill, hover, press, select, scroll, upload, download, switch_tab, screenshot` with `ref` deterministic.

`browser_save_auth` persists `storageState` JSON; pass it back to `browser_open`.
